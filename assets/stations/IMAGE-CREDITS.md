# Station Image Credits & Provenance

Tracking sheet for every built-in station background. Purpose: know where each
image came from and whether we have the right to ship it commercially (Cruise FM
has a paid premium tier, so all art must be cleared for **commercial** use).

**Not legal advice.** This is a working record so nothing ships un-vetted.

## How to fill this in
For each image add: **Source** (exact URL / tool), **License** (e.g. Unsplash
License, Pexels License, "AI — <tool>, commercial tier", or "Pinterest — UNCLEARED"),
and set **Status** to `OK` once it's a licensed/cleared source with no brand marks.

Safe sources: Unsplash, Pexels (free, commercial OK under their license), Adobe
Stock / Shutterstock (paid), or AI from a tool whose terms **grant commercial use**
— and avoid prompts that produce real logos, badges, or licence plates.

## Risk legend
- 🔴 **High** — recognizable trademark/logo/trade-dress in frame, or uncleared Pinterest source. Replace before launch.
- 🟠 **Medium** — stylized brand-like content or AI image with unclear rights. Verify/replace.
- 🟡 **Low** — no brands in frame, but source/license still unconfirmed. Just needs provenance recorded.
