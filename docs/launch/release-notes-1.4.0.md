# What's New — 1.4.0

Paste the block below into App Store Connect → the 1.4.0 version → **What's New
in This Version**. Written for the person downloading it: no build numbers, no
library names, nothing they cannot see.

Apple shows roughly the **first three lines** before "more".

## What this version actually carries

The headline is native, not OTA: Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, and a
real iPad layout. Both need the binary itself — nothing about either ships
over the air. Since the last store release (1.3.1), everything else has been
Apple Music reliability work and detail passes on the modes' visuals.

NOT claimed, deliberately: "shuffle and repeat actually take". The Apple
Music half is real code (26.08) but has never been pressed on a phone, and
the owner's own report (15.09) turned out to be about Spotify, where it is
still open. Custom-station photos are also left out — they shipped in 1.3.1.

## Use this

```
Cruise FM is now on your Home Screen and Lock Screen.

Add a widget for whichever drive matters to you — a spinning record with
your last song, a mirror ball or a CD catching the light, what's on air
right now, or a one-tap Start Drive tile. Pick a look for each one.

Cruise FM also fills the whole screen on iPad now, not just the iPhone.

Also in this update:
• Apple Music plays more reliably — a paused song won't restart when
  you come back to the app, and changing station mid-drive no longer
  trips over itself
• Your custom stations show their photo more brightly behind a drive
• The mirror ball, CD and vinyl record all catch the light more like
  the real thing
```
