# What's New — 1.4.1

Paste the block below into App Store Connect → the 1.4.1 version → **What's New
in This Version**. Written for the person reading it on the update screen: no
build numbers, nothing they cannot see.

Apple shows roughly the **first three lines** before "more".

## What this version actually carries

The headline is Premium — the first version with anything to buy. Two
auto-renewing subscriptions (monthly and annual, both with a 7-day free trial)
unlock the FM stations, the Vinyl / Tuner / Horizon / Mirror Ball / CD modes
and unlimited custom stations. Everything on the AM band, Cassette, Equalizer,
Circular EQ, badges and the share cards stay free.

**And the people who were already here keep Premium for nothing.** Any phone
that used Cruise FM before Premium arrived on it is unlocked for good — decided
on the phone itself, nothing to redeem, nothing that renews. The app tells
them so with a card on the home page (and a one-off notification, where the
phone allows it). That is a real thing they can see, so it earns a line.

NOT claimed: "faster widgets" — the widget-title fix (17.09) is real but is
the kind of thing nobody can go and look at.

## Promotional text

The one field editable without a review. **What is live today says "every one
of them free", which stops being true the moment 1.4.1 is released** — change
it in the same sitting, or the listing contradicts the app.

```
Ten mood stations, eight full-screen modes. Try Premium free for 7 days —
and if you were here first, it's yours already.
```

## The description's FREE section — **must change with this version**

The live description (docs/launch/app-store-listing.md) carries a section that
reads "FREE — Every station and every mode is unlocked. No account needed to
look around, no ads, nothing to buy", and a bullet "Badges and streaks, free
forever". With subscriptions attached to this version, "nothing to buy" is a
false statement on the listing — and misleading metadata is its own rejection,
separate from anything the app does. Replace that section with:

```
FREE TO START, PREMIUM IF YOU WANT MORE
The AM band, the Cassette and Equalizer modes, badges, streaks and share
cards are free, with no account and no ads. Premium unlocks the FM
stations, the Vinyl, Tuner, Horizon, Mirror Ball and CD modes and
unlimited custom stations — try it free for 7 days, then monthly or
yearly. Cancel any time in your Apple ID settings.
```

and change the bullet "Badges and streaks, free forever" to "Badges and
streaks — never behind Premium" (still true, and worth keeping).

Everything else in the description stands.
