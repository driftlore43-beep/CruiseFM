## Full description (max 4000 characters)

> **Spotify organises music by artist. Cruise FM organises it by how a drive
> feels.**
>
> Cruise FM turns your own Spotify playlists into a cinematic driving
> companion. Pick a mood station, hit Start Drive, and your dashboard becomes
> the film your music deserves.
>
> **EIGHT MOOD STATIONS**
> Night Run. Rain Drive. Coastal. Mountain Pass. After Hours. Sunset.
> Cars & Coffee. Tunnel. Every station is a feeling — link any of your own
> Spotify playlists to any station and the drive takes care of the rest.
>
> **FULL-SCREEN VISUAL MODES**
> • Equalizer — a living spectrum tinted to your station's colours
> • Cassette — spinning reels and retro tuner nostalgia
> • Vinyl — your track rotating in warm analogue glow (Premium)
> • Tuner — drag the dial and glide between moods (Premium)
> • Horizon — an endless outrun grid rolling into the sun (Premium)
> • Sound Waves & Circular EQ — pure motion, tuned to the mood (Premium)
>
> **BUILT FOR THE DRIVE**
> • One-tap Start Drive from the home screen
> • Real playback control — play, pause, skip, straight to Spotify
> • Live song titles and progress from your Spotify account
> • Drive stats: drives, hours cruised, streaks — kept honest by our
>   "Are you driving?" check
> • Badges and achievements, free for everyone, forever
>
> **FREE AND PREMIUM**
> Cruise FM is free with the Cassette and Equalizer modes, basic playback,
> and up to three custom stations. Premium (£2.99/month after a 7-day free
> trial, cancel anytime) unlocks every visual mode, every mood theme, and
> unlimited custom stations. Badges and core features are never paywalled.
>
> **GOOD TO KNOW**
> • Cruise FM requires a Spotify Premium account for music playback. It
>   controls your own Spotify app — it does not stream, download, or store
>   music itself.
> • Your data stays on your phone. No Cruise FM account, no server, no
>   selling your listening habits. See our privacy policy.
> • Cruise FM is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed or
>   sponsored by Spotify AB.
>
> Keep your eyes on the road. Cruise FM's visuals are for passengers,
> parked moments, and glances at a mounted phone — never a substitute for
> attentive driving.
>
> Feel the music. Enjoy the ride.
